## Short description

## Full description

Sudoku Wizard turns the classic logic puzzle into a polished magical adventure. Choose Easy, Medium, or Hard puzzles, then tailor each game with Classic, Zen, Timed, and Practice modes.

Complete puzzles to earn Coins, XP, Stars, achievements, and useful power-up tokens. Track your best times and streaks, unlock colorful auras, and return each day for a one-attempt Daily Challenge shared by every player.

Features:

- Easy, Medium, and Hard Sudoku puzzles
- Classic, Zen, Timed, and Practice modes
- Daily Challenge with streak and calendar tracking
- 92 achievements with permanent Coin rewards
- Hint, Auto Notes, and Erase All power-ups
- Pencil notes, undo, number highlighting, and mistake tracking
- Persistent statistics, levels, rewards, and cosmetic auras
- Light and dark themes
- Mouse and touch controls
- Local play with no account, advertising, analytics, or tracking
