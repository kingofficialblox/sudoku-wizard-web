# Sudoku Wizard Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

Sudoku Wizard does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information. The game has no advertising, analytics, account system, or online tracking.

Game progress, settings, achievements, and balances are stored locally on the player's device. On Windows, these files are stored under the current user's local application-data folder in `Sudoku Wizard`. Removing those files resets local progress.

Storefronts used to purchase or download the game may process account and payment information under their own privacy policies. Sudoku Wizard does not receive payment-card details.

Support and privacy questions may be submitted through the project's GitHub issue tracker: https://github.com/kingofficialblox/Sudoku/issues
